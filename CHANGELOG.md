## 0.2.0

first version you can actually install. so this is everything that's in the app so far.

- **sort photos** - give it a folder of reference photos (one per person) and a folder of photos from the day, and it files every photo into a folder per person. photos it can't match go in `_unmatched`. group photos where it only recognized some of the faces also get a copy in `_partial` so you can check them
- **ingest project** - pull a session straight off the phone over wi-fi by scanning a QR code, or import the zip the phone exports
- **send subjects** - send a subject list (CSV) to the phone so you don't have to type everyone in
- **orders** - combine the per-order CSVs from pixieset, copy the ordered photos into one folder for the lab, and build an accounting report from the all-orders export. items with no photo attached (like class photos) get flagged for you to find by hand
- updates itself from here on out
- light and dark mode follow your computer's setting
- the pigeon showed up
